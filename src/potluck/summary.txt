Salvadori is a forward model. He computes what an ISM enriched by one PISN plus Pop II SNII looks like, and then fits individual stars. 2. Is it more complex?

Yes. The effective degrees of freedom go from 3 (plus the dilution parameter) to roughly 12–15: presence and amounts of three or four sources, per-event masses and energies, tt, Z⋆Z⋆​, four table choices, and the observation model.

    Time and fraction are decoupled. Fixing fPISNfPISN​ while varying tt forces M⋆IIM⋆II​ to shrink as tt grows. Drawing amounts avoids that.
