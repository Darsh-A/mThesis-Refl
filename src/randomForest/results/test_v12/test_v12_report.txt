== VERDICT ==
  H3 (real structure) is REFUTED: the shoulders are interpolation artifacts.
  Limongi18 (4_3, L): DOMINANT = H2 mass-grid nodes.  [Ca/Mg] is bimodal
    because the Ca-production turn-on between the 25 and 30 Msun grid models
    makes [Ca/Mg] jump from ~-0.32 (Mg-rich, turnoff mass < ~28 Msun) to
    ~+0.11 (Ca-rich, turnoff mass >= ~28 Msun).  Mass-bin partition collapses
    the distribution to narrow spikes (std ~0.04-0.05) whereas the feh-bin
    partition does not (std 0.13-0.20).  H4 (m_pop2<13 plateau) contributes the
    low-mass regime; H1 (feh interpolation) broadens each mode.
  Nomoto13 (5, N): DOMINANT = H1 metallicity grid (the Z-clamp).  Below feh=-1.13
    (Z < 0.001) the yields are pinned to the Z=0.001 model, so [Ca/Mg] is frozen
    at ~-0.31 -> the dominant shoulder.  H2 (mass-grid) gives only gentle variation
    (Nomoto13 has no sharp Ca turn-on).  feh-bin std is tiny (0.02) below the clamp.
  RANKING: Limongi18 -> H2 > H4 > H1 > H3.  Nomoto13 -> H1(Z-clamp) > H2 > H4 > H3.
  Net: both shoulders are grid artefacts, not real VMP-structure.
